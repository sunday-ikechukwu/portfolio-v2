Sunday Ikechukwu

My research interests lie in robotics, autonomous systems, and intelligent decision-making, particularly in how robots can learn, reason, and operate in complex environments, and how we can enable them to coexist and interact naturally with humans. My broader interests include safe robot learning, intelligent control, human-robot interaction, and autonomous navigation..

I have been exploring these interests through research projects involving learning-based mobile robot navigation, runtime shared autonomy, learning from demonstration (LfD) in robotic manipulation, and human-in-the-loop learning. I am also a Research Assistant at the University of Lagos, working under Dr. Sawyerr. I received my B.Eng. in Mechatronics Engineering from the University of Benin, graduating with First-Class Honours and receiving the Best Graduating Student award in Mechatronics Engineering.
